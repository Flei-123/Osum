# idle budget: two `ps` listings on the idle desktop while the wire chatters (W-W, 18.10.2026)
# 19.10.2026: the kernel dumps the terminal to the serial line only on a pulse (5 s, but 30 s once the desktop is quiet), and a dump
# may come BEFORE the ps output is on screen (or repeat the OLD screen). Fixed sleeps lost the 2nd listing as soon as the idle desktop
# really went quiet (r1188 side effect). Now each ps is followed by an `echo` with a unique word and the script waits until a dump shows
# that word as OUTPUT row `[psdoneN]` -- the ps listing above it is then complete.
warteauf 'launcher: ready' || 120
warte 4
klick 300,200
warte 1
marke \[psdone1\]
tippe ps
taste ret
tippe echo psdone1
taste ret
warteneu \[psdone1\] || 90
warte 25
marke \[psdone2\]
tippe ps
taste ret
tippe echo psdone2
taste ret
warteneu \[psdone2\] || 90
warte 2
