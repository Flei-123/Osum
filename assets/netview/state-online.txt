# ROUND NETVIEW, addendum. Sixteen by sixteen, drawn AT the size it is
# shown at -- a taskbar is 20 to 40 pixels high and an icon designed at
# 24 and squeezed to 16 loses the pixel it was recognisable by.
#
# THE COLOURS ARE ROLES AND NOT VALUES. `@ink` is whatever the running
# scheme calls its foreground, `@dim` its muted text, `@accent` its
# accent, `@warn` its focus colour. So the same file is right in a light
# scheme and in a dark one, and no number in this drawing has to be kept
# in step with /etc/schemas by hand.
#
# AND THE FORM CARRIES THE MEANING, not the colour: a FILLED screen and no badge at all -- nothing is wrong.
# Somebody who cannot tell the accent from the warning colour still sees
# a different SHAPE.
# . =
# I = @ink
# D = @dim
# A = @accent
# W = @warn
