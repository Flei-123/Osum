# at all. A mark on every window would be a screen full of signs that
# say nothing, and the one sign that means something would drown in it.

# knows the next one is about the same thing. What differs is the shape
# INSIDE the ring, never only the colour: a FUNNEL, which lets some through and holds the rest.
